## Reported conjectural patterns

### Odd degrees (d\ge5)

For prime (p), (a\ge1), odd (d\ge5), with
[
m=C_d(p^a),
]
the kickoff target is
[
T_p(m)=p^{ad}+1,
]
equivalently least extremal multiplier
[
q=p^a+1.
]

### Candidate sharp pre-extremal coefficient maximum

The reported candidate is
[
\max_{2\le q\le p^a}
v_p\binom{C_d(p^a)q}{C_d(p^a)}
=\frac{a(d+1)}2.
]

No claim is made here about the corresponding restricted-GCD maximum.

### Cubic boundary

For
[
C_3(p^a)=p^{2a}-p^a+1,
]
the reported conjectural classification is
[
T_p(p^{2a}-p^a+1)=
\begin{cases}
p(p^2-p+1),&a=1,\\
p^{3a}+1,&a\ge2.
\end{cases}
]
